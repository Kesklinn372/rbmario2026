We create a kart to remember

Red Bull Soapbox Race is an amateur race for self-built karts without engines, organized by Red Bull. Participants build creative vehicles from everyday materials and roll down the hill, competing in speed and originality.
The winner is not only the fastest, but also the most spectacular — judges score design and showmanship. It takes place in dozens of cities worldwide, gathers thousands of spectators and has become more of a festival than a sport.
